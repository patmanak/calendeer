Calendeer bridges Proton Calendar and Android's system calendars. Choose which calendars to synchronize, then view events and receive local reminders in your Android calendar apps.

Read-only synchronization is the default. Separate experimental options per calendar enable supported event creation, title changes, description and location changes, date changes and deletion through a compatible Android calendar app. These options have limited supported profiles: invitations, recurrence changes and other unsupported edits may remain pending. Conflicts and uncertain operations are retained for inspection.

Calendeer provides account connection, calendar selection and synchronization settings. It has no built-in event editor or calendar browser and does not deliver reminder notifications itself. An Android calendar app is needed to view or edit mirrored events and handle local reminders.

Requires Android 12 or later and a Proton account. Synchronization depends on the Proton service and an Internet connection; already mirrored events remain available in your calendar apps offline. Calendeer is an independent project and is not affiliated with Proton.
