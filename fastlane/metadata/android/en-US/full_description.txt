Use your Android calendar app to view Proton Calendar events, edit supported events and receive reminders. Calendeer syncs the calendars you choose. It requires Android 12 or later and a Proton account.

Each calendar starts in Read-only mode. Choose Editable for a writable personal calendar to create, edit or delete ordinary events, including all-day events, reminders and event colors. You can also create and edit a whole finite daily series without individual exceptions. Changing one occurrence, deleting a series and sending guest invitations are still in development.

Events already on the phone remain available offline. Calendeer keeps pending changes and shows when a sync needs attention. The selected events are copied into Android’s Calendar Provider, where other apps with calendar permission can read them.

Calendeer is an independent open-source project.
